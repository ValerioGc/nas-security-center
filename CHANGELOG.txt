# Changelog

All notable changes to nas-security-center are documented in this file.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- The texts of the interface, in Italian and English, are impersonal and shorter: no ellipsis,
  colon or semicolon, no names of internal programs, commands or variables.
- The scrollbars come from the shared library, which shows only their thumb: no arrows and no
  track.
- The retry of an error banner sits on its right, whatever the length of the reason.
- Certificates. The state of the connection is a banner with its tone and sign: information when
  HTTPS is off, a warning on HTTP or on an address the certificate does not cover, a success when
  it covers it. The download of the authority is the main action, without underline.
- Certificates. The guide to trust the authority has a tab for each system, with its browsers and
  numbered steps, rewritten.
- Headings, fields, page header, panels and hidden labels come from `_nas-controls.scss` of the
  shared library instead of a copy of the same rules; `.surface_card` from `_nas-apps.scss`.

### Fixed

- Certificates. "Apri in HTTPS" did nothing: the frame guard of the application cancels a link to
  the page that holds it. The button now asks the launcher (`openHttps`, message `open-https`) to
  load itself again over HTTPS.

### Removed

- The `serde` dependency of the backend, unused: only `serde_json` is.

## [0.1.12]

### Added

- The switch of the discovery by NAS Finder in the access section (requests `discovery-settings`
  and `save-discovery-settings` of the launcher): the wizard turns it off when it is done, and it
  stays off until the system is reset unless it is turned on here. A refused change puts the
  switch back and says why.
- The page opens on the section the launcher asks for (`?section=access`, from the link of a
  notification); an unknown one opens the access, as before.
- The kinds of scan are selectable cards, one chosen at a time, started by their button.
- Placeholders while the state is read, for an empty quarantine, a clean scan and an antivirus
  that is off; a progress bar with stripes, its percentage beside it, and a running stripe when
  the progress is unknown.
- A custom scan can ignore the files unchanged since the last scan (off by default); the quick
  scan always does, and the page says so. The result shows how many files were skipped, the
  progress how many of the planned ones were checked, and the Scans tab of the settings forgets
  the files already scanned.

### Changed

- The files of `assets/`, with a hash of their content in the name, are served with
  `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, the page with `no-cache`: the browser keeps
  the scripts and the styles between visits instead of asking for them again through the gateway
  and the launcher.
- The settings icon is larger and on the other side of the title. The settings have four tabs:
  General (turning the antivirus on, or off after a confirmation: no more on the page), Schedule,
  Scans (one card for each kind) and Quarantine; while it is off only General.
- The launcher bridge (under `askNasShell`), the shared styles, icons, switches, number steppers,
  the native dialog under `CommonModal`, the power schedule ranges and the build, package and
  release scripts come from the shared library `@valeriogc/nas-base-app`. A number typed out of its
  range now shows again the value kept, which never changed.

## [0.1.11]

### Changed

- In the frame of the launcher the application keeps the rules of its old sandbox by itself
  (`src/services/frameGuard.ts`, called first in `main.ts`): no window of its own, no
  `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` dialog, no navigation of the page that holds it. The launcher dropped
  the sandbox, which on its origin was no wall and made the browser warn: nothing else changes.
- Antivirus comes right after Devices in the side menu.
- The errors show their whole reason, wrapped and scrolling when long, beside their retry; the
  Antivirus page keeps its button as wide as its text (primary to enable, red to disable) and
  sets its progress apart.
- The text on the accent follows the theme (`--on-accent`), readable on the bright accent of the
  dark theme.

## [0.1.3]

### Added

- The real devices section: access with a certificate only (mTLS), turned on and off with the
  account password, and the devices allowed in, each with a name, an icon and its certificate.
- A new device gets its own certificate, downloaded with the password of its file and the authority
  of the NAS; a device is renamed, its certificate renewed or revoked, and it can be removed.

### Removed

- The sample devices.

### Changed

- The surfaces follow the theme chosen in the launcher for its type, through the colours of the
  context; outside the launcher the application keeps its own.
